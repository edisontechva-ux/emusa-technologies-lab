# Manage Safe Links

## Overview

Safe Links helps protect users from malicious URLs by scanning and validating links contained in email messages and Microsoft 365 applications. Microsoft Defender for Office 365 rewrites URLs and performs real-time verification when users click links to help prevent access to unsafe websites.

## Location

**Microsoft Defender Portal → Email & Collaboration → Policies & Rules → Threat Policies → Safe Links**

## Steps

1. Signed in to the Microsoft Defender Portal using an administrator account.
2. Navigated to **Email & Collaboration**.
3. Selected **Policies & Rules**.
4. Opened **Threat Policies**.
5. Selected **Safe Links**.
6. Reviewed the existing Safe Links policies.
7. Selected **Create Policy**.
8. Entered the Safe Links policy name.
9. Entered the policy description.
10. Configured Safe Links protection settings.
11. Enabled URL scanning and protection.
12. Reviewed click protection settings.
13. Configured Microsoft Teams protection settings.
14. Configured Office application protection settings.
15. Selected the target users, groups, or domains.
16. Reviewed the policy configuration.
17. Selected **Create**.
18. Verified that the Safe Links policy was successfully created.
19. Confirmed that Safe Links protection was enabled for the assigned recipients.

## Result

A Safe Links policy was successfully configured in Microsoft Defender for Office 365. Protected users will have URLs scanned and evaluated to help prevent access to malicious websites.

## Administrative Notes

Safe Links provides time-of-click protection against malicious URLs delivered through email messages and Microsoft 365 services.

URL protection remains effective even if a website becomes malicious after the original message is delivered.

Administrators should regularly review Safe Links policies to ensure protection settings align with organizational security requirements.
