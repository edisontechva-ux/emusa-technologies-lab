# Configure App Permission Policies

## Overview

App permission policies control which Microsoft Teams apps users can install, access, and use within the organization.

## Location

**Microsoft Teams admin center → Teams apps → Permission policies**

## Steps

1. Opened the **Microsoft Teams admin center**.
2. Navigated to **Teams apps → Permission policies**.
3. Reviewed the available permission policies.
4. Selected **Add**.
5. Entered a policy name and description.
6. Configured Microsoft apps permissions.
7. Configured third-party app permissions.
8. Configured custom app permissions.
9. Reviewed the policy settings.
10. Selected **Save** to create the policy.
11. Confirmed that the policy appeared in the policy list.
12. Opened the policy and reviewed the configured settings.
13. Assigned the policy to a test user where applicable.
14. Verified that the policy was successfully applied.

## Result

The app permission policy was successfully created and configured. Application access was controlled according to organizational requirements.

## Administrative Notes

App permission policies help control which applications users can access in Microsoft Teams.

Policies should be reviewed periodically to ensure they align with organizational security, governance, and compliance requirements.

Only approved applications should be made available to users.
