# Edit Team

## Overview

Editing a Microsoft Team allows administrators to update team settings, descriptions, privacy options, and team information to reflect changing business requirements.

## Location

**Microsoft Teams admin center → Teams → Manage teams**

## Steps

1. Opened the **Microsoft Teams admin center**.
2. Navigated to **Teams → Manage teams**.
3. Located the team to be modified.
4. Selected the team name.
5. Opened the team settings and properties.
6. Updated the team description.
7. Reviewed the team privacy setting.
8. Reviewed the team owners and members.
9. Selected **Apply** to save the changes.
10. Confirmed that the updated information was successfully applied.
11. Reopened the team properties and verified the changes.

## Result

The Microsoft Team was successfully updated, and the new team settings and information were applied.

## Administrative Notes

Team information should be reviewed periodically to ensure it remains accurate and aligned with business requirements.

Only authorized administrators and team owners should make configuration changes.

Changes made in the Teams admin center may require a short period to synchronize across Microsoft Teams.
