# Create Team

## Overview

A Microsoft Team provides a workspace for collaboration, chat, meetings, file sharing, and communication between users.

## Location

**Microsoft Teams admin center → Teams → Manage teams**

## Steps

1. Opened the **Microsoft Teams admin center**.
2. Navigated to **Teams → Manage teams**.
3. Selected **Add**.
4. Entered the team name and description.
5. Configured the privacy setting.
6. Selected **Apply** to create the team.
7. Waited for Microsoft Teams to provision the team.
8. Confirmed that the team appeared in the team list.
9. Opened the newly created team and reviewed its settings.

## Result

The Microsoft Team was successfully created and is available for collaboration, meetings, chat, and file sharing.

## Administrative Notes

Teams can be configured as either **Private** or **Public** depending on business requirements.

Each team should have at least one owner responsible for membership management and team governance.

After creation, channels, members, apps, and policies can be configured as needed.
