# Assign License to User

Licenses in Microsoft 365 control which apps and services a user account can access — Exchange (mail), Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, and more. Licenses are assigned per user from the user's profile in the admin center, either as a full plan or with individual apps/services toggled on or off.

---

# Location

**Microsoft 365 admin center → Users → Active users → [select user] → Licenses and Apps tab**

Opening a user's profile and switching to the Licenses and Apps tab shows all available license plans in the tenant, along with a breakdown of which individual apps/services each plan includes.

---

# Steps

1. Opened the Active users list and selected **Andres Navarro**.
2. Went to the **Licenses and Apps** tab on his profile.
3. Selected the **Microsoft 365 Business Premium** license.
4. Expanded the plan to review individual app/service toggles, and confirmed Email (Exchange), sign-out of all sessions capability, and group management access were enabled as part of the assignment.
5. Clicked **Save changes**.
6. Confirmed the license and its associated apps now show as active on the user's profile.

---

# Result

Andres Navarro was successfully licensed with Microsoft 365 Business Premium. No issues encountered during assignment — the license and its apps applied immediately and were confirmed active on the profile.

# Administrative Notes

Assigning at the plan level (rather than per-app) is the simplest approach for standard users, but reviewing the app toggles on the Licenses and Apps tab is worth doing during assignment — it's an easy point to catch or intentionally exclude services a user shouldn't have (e.g. disabling Teams for a role that doesn't need it) without needing a separate step later.
